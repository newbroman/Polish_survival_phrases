📚 Levels & Dictionary

Tap 📚 (top-right) to open this panel — the heart of the app:

🗂️ STUDY page

Flashcards to meet new phrases. On each card:

🔊Listen — tap to hear it; tap again (2× / 3×) to slow it right down.
🎤Speak — hold and say it; you'll get a pronunciation score.
🔄🇵🇱→🇬🇧 toggle (red outline, shows flags) flips which language is on top.
⭐⭐ Save to Sandbox  ·  💡 Hint reveals grammar notes.
👈Swipe the card (or tap ◀ ▶) to move through the deck.

🗂️ PRACTICE page

Quiz yourself — the phrase plays, you tap the matching tile.

🔄🇵🇱→🇬🇧 toggle (red outline): by default the prompt is Polish and the tiles are English — tap to reverse.
🔊Replay the phrase (tap again to slow it).
⏸️Pause the round timer when you need a break.
⭐Save the current phrase to your Sandbox.
🏆Three correct = mastered. Fast answers score more!

🗂️ PHRASES page

Browse every phrase in the level and track progress.

🔊Each phrase has its own Listen button (tap again to slow).
🎤Hold the mic on a phrase to practise it and see a score — nothing is marked.
🔢Badges show your score; Due / Needs Work flag what to revisit.

🎯 Spaced Repetition (SRS) & Level R

True Spaced Repetition: Words you master are now scheduled for review at optimal intervals (1 day, 3 days, 7 days, etc.) so you never forget them.

Level R (Daily Due): This is your most important level! It automatically fills with words that you made a mistake on recently, AND any mastered words that are statistically due for a review today.

Mastery Counters: Every time you fully master a level and choose to "Repeat" it, your completion counter (🔄x1) goes up!

🌍 Browser Compatibility
  • Apple / iOS: Please use Safari. Other browsers block the microphone and text-to-speech due to Apple's security rules.
  • Android: Please use Google Chrome for the best native Voice Recognition support.
  • Note: You must grant microphone permissions when prompted.
⚙️ Installing High-Quality Voices

For natural sounding Polish, you must download the native voices to your device OS:

🍎 Apple (iPhone/iPad):
  1. Go to Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content.
  2. Tap Voices, then find Polish.
  3. Download and select Zosia (Enhanced or Premium) or Krzysztof.
  4. Crucial: Make sure your phone's physical "Silent/Ring" switch is set to Ring!
🤖 Android:
  1. Go to Settings > General Management (or System).
  2. Tap Text-to-speech output.
  3. Ensure Speech Services by Google is the preferred engine.
  4. Tap the gear icon ⚙️ > Install voice data > Polish.
🎤 Voice Recognition & Controls

Tap 🔊 to listen (tap again 2× / 3× to slow it down), and hold 🎤 to speak — you'll get a pronunciation score. These sit on the Study card, on each Phrases-tab phrase, and the 🔊 is in the Practice header.

🔄 Language toggle: the red-outlined button flips the direction. It shows flags — 🇵🇱→🇬🇧 means a Polish prompt with English answers (the default), and 🇬🇧→🇵🇱 is the reverse. It's on the Study card and the Practice header, and your choice is remembered.

🧠 Hard Mode: Hides the written text entirely so you rely purely on listening. You can trigger this mode after mastering any level.

⏱️ Scoring, Timers & Sandbox

Points: +10 for fast correct. +6 if over 4 seconds. -5 for mistakes.
Timer (12s): If you take longer than 12 seconds to answer, the app assumes you were distracted. You will get 0 points and lose your streak!
Daily Goal: Hit 1000 points daily (~15 mins of practice) to build your 🔥 Daily Streak! Missing a day resets it to 0.
Win Streaks: Answering correctly in a row raises the audio pitch and triggers confetti at streaks of 5, 25, 50, and 70!

Sandbox: Tap the 🔍 icon to search for a word. Use Translate to build a list in "Lvl C". Export Lvl C from the settings gear icon to permanently save your list.
